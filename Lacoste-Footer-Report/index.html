🔍 Root Cause
Confirmed via direct live testing (submitting the newsletter signup form with a fresh and with a repeated email, and screenshotting before/after): the footer's "Member Registration" signup form gives literally zero visible feedback on submission — no success message, no error message, no "already registered" message, nothing changes on screen at all. The keyword-based verify_form_response check was previously scoped with search_whole_page:true, which caused a false PASS: the word "already" happens to appear elsewhere on the page (an unrelated "Sign in / Sign up — Already a member?" account panel that is NOT inside <footer> and has nothing to do with the newsletter form), so the check matched that coincidental text within its first 0ms poll regardless of whether the signup form did anything. Scoping the search to <footer> only (removing search_whole_page) removes that false positive and lets the test accurately reflect the real behavior: the form never produces any detectable response, so the check now correctly times out and fails.
